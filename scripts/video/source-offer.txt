Viewer distributes the dynamically linked ViewerVideoRuntime components under
their recorded licenses. For at least three years after a Viewer binary release,
the exact corresponding source archives, build scripts, and relinkable Viewer
source are available from the release source tree.

Reproduce source acquisition and the reviewed build from the repository root:

  node scripts/video/runtime-lock.mjs list scripts/video/runtime.lock.json
  export VIEWER_VIDEO_WORK_DIR="$(mktemp -d)"
  export VIEWER_VIDEO_STAGE_DIR="$(pwd)/target/viewer-video-runtime/$(uname -m | sed 's/arm64/aarch64/')-apple-darwin/ViewerVideoRuntime"
  scripts/video/build-macos-runtime.sh
  scripts/video/verify-runtime.sh

`runtime-lock.mjs list` prints every exact source URL and SHA-256 digest before
the build script downloads and independently verifies those archives. The lock,
build script, and Viewer source are the preferred form for modification.
