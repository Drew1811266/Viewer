Each stable requirement ID owns every acceptance statement beneath its tagged Product Spec heading until the next tagged heading. A row may span stages when an early milestone supplies the behavior and Continuous captures ongoing regression checks. No row can be declared complete from a prototype gate alone; the stage test named here must exercise the integrated product path.

## Stage ownership summary

- **M1–M3:** completed historical product milestones.
- **Continuous:** constraints and regression checks applied to ordinary development.
- **Future:** unimplemented product behavior requiring a separate future design and plan.
- **NotApplicable:** release/distribution acceptance outside the current development stage.
